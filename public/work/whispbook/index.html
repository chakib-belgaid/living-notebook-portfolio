01

Context and problem

Turning a book into a listenable audiobook usually means uploading private reading material, waiting for the whole book to render, and losing control over voices, chapters and corrections. Whisperbook keeps import, attribution, synthesis, playback and export on the device.

03

Architecture and data flow

A single-module Kotlin/Compose app copies the selected publication into private storage, extracts EPUB or PDF text, detects chapters and speakers, persists the library in Room, and prepares opening audio through WorkManager. Supertonic 3 generates finalized local WAV segments that Media3 can play progressively while later chapters continue preparing.

  1. Local EPUB or PDF
  2. Private extraction and chapter detection
  3. Speaker attribution and voice cast
  4. Progressive Supertonic 3 synthesis
  5. Media3 playback and synchronized passages
  6. Offline book-level MP3 export

Inside Whisperbook

Four steps from a book to its audio.

  1. Book You import your own EPUB or PDF on the phone. Privacy holds at the system boundary: the app has no runtime network permission.
  2. Chapters Chapters and their speakers are found in the text. Audio is prepared in small segments, so the opening chapter plays while later ones are still being narrated.
  3. Voices Each speaker is given a different local voice, so a conversation reads as a cast. Narration runs on the device.
  4. Audio Playback follows the text as it plays, and the finished book can be exported as an MP3.

04

Engineering decisions

Remove runtime networking entirely.

The Android manifest has no INTERNET or ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE permission, so imported books, generated audio and listening history stay inside the device boundary.Alternative not selected: Requiring a hosted model, account or cloud fallback for core narration.

Stream finalized local segments instead of waiting for the whole book.

The opening chapter becomes listenable first while later chapters are prepared sequentially, without sacrificing cache, seek or restart behavior.Alternative not selected: Blocking playback until every chapter has been synthesized.

05

Reliability, security and evaluation

  • Room, DataStore and WorkManager persist library, settings and preparation checkpoints across restarts.
  • Finalized WAV microsegments remain the common cache, playback and export representation.
  • JVM and connected Android tests cover import, synthesis, playback, navigation, voice selection and chapter continuation on the production Compose surfaces.
